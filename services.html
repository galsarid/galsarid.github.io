Consulting and collaboration

Ways I can contribute.

My background spans astrophysics, planetary science, geoscience, remote sensing, physical modeling, and scientific computing. That breadth is most useful on problems where the science question, the measurement, the model, and the interpretation all have to be considered together.

I’m open to exploratory conversations about consulting, advisory work, or collaboration. I don’t promise current availability, and any outside work has to be compatible with my employer obligations.

Physical modeling

Scientific modeling and simulation

Develop or assess physical and numerical models, examine assumptions and sensitivities, and connect model behavior to the underlying science.

Measurements

Measurements, calibration, and uncertainty

Work from observations or experimental measurements to physical interpretation, including calibration, validation, uncertainty, and what the data can actually support.

Data and software

Data analysis and scientific computing

Build or review quantitative workflows for analysis, simulation, visualization, and reproducible scientific work.

Review and planning

Technical review and science planning

Evaluate scientific concepts, assumptions, measurement strategies, trade studies, and research plans, especially when a problem crosses disciplinary boundaries.

Availability: exploratory discussions are welcome; availability is not guaranteed. Scope, timing, conflicts, and any applicable employer requirements would need to be resolved before any engagement.
